OUR STORY

Life gets busy.
Laundry can be simpler.

There is always another meeting, another errand or another full basket. Laundry Guru starts with that everyday reality: you want clean clothes without making laundry the centre of your day.

Choose the clothes you have, see how the price is worked out, and pick the care and turnaround that suit you. It is a straightforward way to plan your next wash.

For your home. For your business.

Everyday clothes, household laundry and special outfits all need a different kind of attention. So do the towels, linen and uniforms used by hostels, salons and small guesthouses.

For a regular business order, get in touch through our social pages to discuss the item list, collection schedule and a quote.
