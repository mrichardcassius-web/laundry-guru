Pickup & delivery

Pickup areas are Nakawa, Ntinda and Bugolobi in Kampala. Pickup and return together are estimated at UGX 10,000. Bringing and collecting clothes yourself has no transport fee. Confirm the meeting point and availability with the team before travelling.

Express aims for 4 opening hours with a 50% cleaning surcharge. Standard aims for 24 hours at the listed rate. Economy aims for 48 hours with 10% off cleaning. These are targets after receipt at the shop, inspection and payment review; Sunday closure and special fabrics may extend the time. Transport is not a cleaning-speed guarantee.

Confirm the pickup partner, meeting point, item count and return time directly with the team. The website does not book a driver.

Clothes, care & pricing

Read each garment’s care label. Check stains, pockets and existing damage with the team, count the items and agree on any price changes before work starts. Remove valuables from pockets.

Washing may be priced by item, kilogram or basket. A basket holds up to 6 kg. Jackets, suits, bedding and wedding gowns are priced per item. Dry cleaning and ironing are per item. Bras, knickers, boxers and towels are wash-only in this catalogue. Ironing accepts clean, dry clothes.

Displayed prices are estimates. The total includes your chosen cleaning, turnaround and transport. Confirm the final amount with the team after garment inspection. Provider fees, if any, are shown by your mobile money provider.

Problems, cancellations & refunds

Report a delay, missing item or cleaning issue using your laundry reference and a clear description. The team can check the intake record and discuss a remedy such as re-cleaning, repair or an agreed refund.

Before cleaning starts, a verified deposit would be refundable, less any transport already agreed and used. After cleaning begins, refunds would depend on the work done and the issue. A refund would go back through the verified original payment route, with a receipt. No real refunds happen on this site.

Privacy notice

The website stores clothing choices, quantities, pickup area, turnaround, transaction reference, order code and complaint details in this browser. It does not request your name, phone number, email, home address, PIN or account login.

Why: to keep your choices between steps and find a saved record later. Who can see it: anyone using this browser profile. The laundry team does not receive these form entries. No analytics or advertising scripts are included. The font is stored with the website.

How long: records stay until you clear them below or clear the browser’s site data. Browser storage is not an encrypted customer database. Your browser and hosting provider may keep ordinary access logs under their own policies.

Before live bookings: contact and delivery details would need a shared, secure order system and a reviewed privacy process. It should explain what information is needed, who handles it, how long it is kept and how customers request access or deletion. That live service is not connected to this preview.

Simple terms

Confirm your order, collection arrangements, final price and recipient name with Laundry Guru before making a payment. Entering a transaction ID on this website does not verify payment or send an order to the team. A saved receipt is a record of the details entered, not proof of payment or acceptance of a booking.

For the business model, a 50% deposit would be due after the shop confirms the order; the balance would be due before return. Full payment is another option. Staff would verify payments against their own provider records, not screenshots or a transaction ID alone.
