How to pay

MTN MoMo Mobile money

Merchant code: 42614790

Confirm the recipient name displayed by your provider before approving.

  1. Open the official MTN MoMo app or mobile money menu and choose merchant payment.
  2. Enter merchant code 42614790 and the agreed amount. Check the recipient name and any provider fee before approving.
  3. Keep the transaction ID from your confirmation. Enter it with your order reference so the team can check its own payment records.
Airtel Money Mobile money

Merchant code: 7041788

Confirm the recipient name displayed by your provider before approving.

  1. Open the official Airtel Money app or mobile money menu and choose merchant payment.
  2. Enter merchant code 7041788 and the agreed amount. Check the recipient name and any provider fee before approving.
  3. Keep the transaction ID from your confirmation. Enter it with your order reference so the team can check its own payment records.

How would we confirm payment?

The laundry owner would check their own mobile money or payment-provider records and match the amount, date and transaction ID. A screenshot or typed ID alone is never proof. This website cannot check provider records.

Our refund rule

Before cleaning starts, a confirmed deposit would be refundable, less any transport already agreed and used. After work starts, the shop would review the issue and agree a fair remedy. Read the full rules .
